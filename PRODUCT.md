# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
People who scroll a lot, mostly on social, and post through Buffer: creators, marketers, small-business owners. They want to keep a posting streak going and would like their screen time to feel a bit kinder. Secondary audience: Buffer Commit judges, who give it a couple of minutes.

## Product Purpose
Sproutling is a pixel pet that lives in the browser, is fed by Buffer posting activity, and offers gentle, never-scolding reminders to pause and look up from the scroll. The landing page has to get a visitor to understand that in about 20 seconds, let them meet the pet (it wanders the page and can be walked with WASD), and get them to Add to Chrome.

## Brand Personality
Playful, retro, cozy. Warm and a little quirky, with a calm voice. A small companion that looks after your attention instead of hooking it.

## Anti-references
- Neon pixel-arcade cliche: dark mode, CRT scanlines, glowing neon. The pixel art is the charm, not a theme.
- Gamified streak and guilt apps: shame, flames, urgency, red alerts. Sproutling never scolds.
- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient hero, identical feature cards, logo wall, metrics strip.

## Design Principles
- Companion, not product: the pet is the star and everything else is its home.
- Kind by default: no urgency, no pressure, nothing that nags, in copy or in motion.
- Show it, don't list it: fewer sprites doing real work beat many decorative ones.
- Pixel crispness everywhere: integer scales, stepped corners, no blur.
- Calm motion: one thing moves at a time, and all of it can be switched off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast in light and dark. Full keyboard use. Reduced motion respected (no wandering, no drifting decoration). The pet's single-key controls can be switched off with Esc.
